ClipRelay remote desktop components

GPL-3.0. See COPYING. Its exact release, download hash and corresponding source

Sunshine's tools/sunshinesvc.cpp and is also GPL-3.0-only. Its complete source,
local control client, installation/build scripts and integration are included in
ClipRelay's source repository:
https://github.com/ffffhx/cliprelay
